Four accounts, then stop thinking about it

Every dollar goes to the next unfilled bucket. Set each one to auto-transfer on payday so the decision happens once, not fifty-two times a year.

  1. 401(k), but only up to the match

    If your employer matches, that match is an instant 50–100% return — nothing else on this page comes close. Find out this week what the match is and contribute exactly that much. If there's no match, skip straight to step 2.

    ask HRhighest priority

  2. Cash cushion — three months of bills

    In a high-yield savings account, separate from checking, no debit card attached. This is what stops a bad month from becoming credit card debt. Fill it once, then leave it alone.

    $5,445target

  3. Roth IRA — max it every year

    You open it yourself at Fidelity, Schwab, or Vanguard in about fifteen minutes. Money goes in after tax and every dollar of growth comes out tax-free at retirement. At your income this is the single best account available to you. Opening it is not investing — buy an index fund inside it, or it just sits in cash.

    $583per month

  4. Regular brokerage for the rest

    Same brokerage, ordinary taxable account, no contribution limit. One total-market index fund is a complete portfolio — VTI, VOO, FXAIX or SWTSX all do the same job for near-zero fees. Buy it automatically every payday and never look at it.

    $1,417per month

The part people get wrong

The money you put in is the small half. At your dial setting you contribute $864,000 over your working life and end with $3.9M — the other $3.0M is growth you did nothing to earn except start early and not touch it.

That gap is entirely a function of time, which is the one input you can't buy more of later. Waiting five years to start costs you more than any raise you're likely to get.

Two things this page assumes

7% a year is a historical average, not a promise. That's roughly what a broad US index fund has returned above inflation over the long run — so these figures are in today's buying power. Real years are lumpy: some are −20%. The average only shows up if you stay in through the bad ones.

Check the current Roth IRA limit before you set the transfer. The field above defaults to $7,000; the IRS adjusts it for inflation, so confirm this year's number and update it.
