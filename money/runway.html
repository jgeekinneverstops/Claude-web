The real reason to open one

It's not the interest — at your balance that's about $3 a month. It's friction. Money in a separate bank with no debit card attached and a one-day transfer delay is money you don't spend on a Friday. That separation is worth more to you than the yield.

Pick any of Ally, Marcus, SoFi, Capital One 360, or Wealthfront — they're all in the same 3.5–4.5% range and the difference between them is rounding error. Do not put it in a CD or anything with a withdrawal penalty while your runway is under three months. You need this reachable.
