The question

Can $1,200 pay you $2,500 a week?

Not from investing it. Paying yourself $2,500 a week out of $1,200 means earning back more than twice your entire balance every seven days, forever. No market, fund, or asset does that. Anything advertising it is a scam.

What $1,200 can do is buy tools, inventory, or a first month of costs for a small operating business — where the money you make comes from work you sell, not from a return on the balance. That path to $2,500 a week is real, and it is measured in years, not weeks. This ledger tracks both sides honestly: what your capital earns, and what your ventures earn.

Figures update live from the numbers you enter. Venture estimates are typical U.S. owner-operator ranges, not promises — your market, pricing, and hours will move them. Nothing here is financial advice.
